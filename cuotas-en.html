Membership

Fees and membership

Be part of the free-flight community in Jalisco. Your membership gives you access to the launch sites managed by the association and to all of our projects.

  • Under 18s fly free of charge
  • Voting rights at assemblies
  • Join the committees of the Club de Vuelo Libre Jalisco
  • Unlimited use of the Las Antenas launch site
  • Unlimited use of the Piano and Los Papalotes landing zones
  • Access to the first-aid kit at Los Papalotes
  • Does not include the Gravedad 0 and La Ceja launch sites (these have a separate fee)

Refund policy

We have a 30-day refund policy: you have 30 days after making your payment to request a refund.

To be eligible for a refund, you will need your proof of payment, and the membership, fee or paid flight must not have been used.

To start a refund, you can contact us at pagos@vuelolibrejalisco.com.

We will notify you whether the refund was approved or not. If approved, you will receive it on your original payment method within 10 business days. Please note that your bank or credit card company may also take some time to process and reflect the refund.

If more than 15 business days have passed since we approved your refund, contact us again at pagos@vuelolibrejalisco.com.

Questions or concerns about your payment? Write to us at pagos@vuelolibrejalisco.com.
