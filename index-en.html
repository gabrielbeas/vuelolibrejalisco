Pilots

Welcome · Bienvenido · Bienvenue

Since 1978 we have brought together those who fly paragliders and hang gliders in Jalisco — from the Tapalpa sierras to the Yelapa coast. Whether you fly with us for a single day or for many years, you will find an organized community, well-kept sites and people willing to share the sky.

Your flight, your responsibility

Flying in Jalisco is a privilege we share with landowners, local communities and other users of the airspace. That is why we say it plainly: every pilot is responsible for knowing and respecting the rules of the site where they fly.

  • The procedures and restrictions of each launch site
  • The applicable airspace and current notices
  • Authorized landing zones and private land
  • Your own level, equipment, license and insurance

The club organizes, manages access and informs — but it does not supervise or authorize individual flights. The decision to launch is always yours, and so are its consequences. If you have any doubt about a site, ask before you fly: there will always be someone in the community willing to explain it to you.

Visiting pilots

Are you coming from another state or another country? We would be glad to host you. Here is what to sort out before your first flight with us.

Arrival checklist

  • Write to us before you travel
  • Contact the local pilot at the site
  • Bring your license and insurance
  • Pay the visitor fee
  • Attend the briefing before launching

Visitor fees

  • $100 MXN per day
  • $1,000 MXN for 15 days
  • Payment available via MercadoPago
  • See all fees →

Before you come

  • Check that your insurance covers Mexico
  • Check the level required at each site
