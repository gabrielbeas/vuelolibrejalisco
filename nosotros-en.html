About Us

We bring together and organize free-flight practitioners in the state of Jalisco.

Who we are

The Club de Vuelo Libre de Jalisco has brought together and organized free-flight practitioners in the state since 1978. We manage access to the sites, look after our relationship with landowners and communities, and maintain a committee structure that shares the work among those who fly here.

Mission and vision

Mission

To build a united and participatory community that drives the organized growth of free flight and protects the people and places where we practice our passion.

Vision

To be a human, representative and transparent institution, recognized for strengthening the flying community, driving sustainable growth and securing the continuity of, and access to, our flying sites for future generations.
